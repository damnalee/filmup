# Brag Plan: FilmUp

## What is this app?
FilmUp helps people in Lahore discover cinemas, browse current and upcoming movies, find future showtimes, and opt in to advance-booking alerts.

## The angle
A movie night in Lahore should not require ten tabs and guesswork. FilmUp puts the city, cinemas and real showtime data on one map — then watches for advance-booking openings when a visitor asks it to.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark, cinematic map snaps into focus with the line: **"Lahore. Movie night. Sorted."** The real FilmUp interface is visible immediately.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The live Lahore map and cinema pins: "Every cinema. One map."
- Movie discovery and actual future showtime cards: "Real listings. Real showtimes."
- The upcoming-movie alert flow: choose cinemas, confirm email, get a heads-up when advance booking opens.

## Outro / punchline
FilmUp wordmark, then: **"Find the show. Find the cinema."** End on a clean, memorable brand lockup.

## User flow worth showing
Open FilmUp → explore cinemas and movies on the map → inspect showtimes or subscribe to an advance-booking alert for a coming-soon movie.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium city-night product film; confident, precise and distinctly Lahore-focused
- Interpretation: cinematic pacing, restrained typography, purposeful transitions and no exaggerated claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: deep near-black/navy, based on the app's dark interface
- Accent: warm amber/gold used by the FilmUp wordmark and interface highlights
- Text: warm white
- Display font: app wordmark treatment; use a clean geometric sans for motion text
- Body font: app's existing UI sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: real interactive map with cinema pins, movie cards and the FilmUp chrome wordmark

## Share copy (draft)
Lahore movie nights, mapped. Find cinemas, check real showtimes and get an alert when advance booking opens — FilmUp brings it together.

## Audio direction
- Role: polished cinematic support
- Music: upbeat, modern instrumental bed with a warm low-end and light percussive pulse; use only licensed/bundled assets
- Music treatment: quick, controlled fade-in; support the map reveal and final brand lockup; soft fade-out
- Music cue guidance: Vol. 12 cue preset: tempo ~110 BPM; strong cues at 8.74s, 13.11s and 17.47s; use 13.11s for the alert reveal and 17.47s for the final wordmark
- Audio-reactive treatment: none; clarity over visualizers
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched UI ticks and soft transition accents
- Audio-coupled moments: map reveal, cinema-pin sequence and final wordmark
- Restraint rule: no trailer booms, no noisy interface sounds, no narration required

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The city, in focus — 3s
A dark field resolves into the actual FilmUp map interface. The map and Lahore cinema pins become visible. Text: "LAHORE. MOVIE NIGHT. SORTED."
Sequential/interaction: map reveal, then three restrained pin pulses.
Audio intent: establish confidence and scale.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft impact as the map resolves.
Transition mood: cinematic wipe → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — One map, many cinemas — 4s
Show the actual app screenshot and a subtle camera push toward the cinema pins. Text: "EVERY CINEMA. ONE MAP."
Sequential/interaction: pin highlights appear one by one.
Audio intent: create momentum without clutter.
Audio-coupled idea: light ticks on the pin highlights.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Find the show — 5s
Show movie discovery and showtime information from the product UI. Text: "REAL LISTINGS. REAL SHOWTIMES."
Sequential/interaction: movie card and time options reveal in sequence; do not invent prices or session times.
Audio intent: reassuring, precise.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet UI click on the reveal.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Don't miss the booking window — 5s
Recreate the real upcoming-movie notification flow: choose a film, select cinemas, confirm email, receive an alert when matching sessions appear. Text: "GET THE HEADS-UP WHEN ADVANCE BOOKING OPENS."
Sequential/interaction: cinema choices tick on; confirmation state follows. Do not display real subscriber details or email addresses.
Audio intent: a sense of anticipation and payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: gentle confirmation tone.
Transition mood: dramatic clean cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — FilmUp — 3s
A clean FilmUp brand lockup on the dark brand background. Text: "FIND THE SHOW. FIND THE CINEMA." Then the FilmUp wordmark.
Sequential/interaction: tagline settles, then wordmark.
Audio intent: confident close.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft brand accent and short fade.
Transition mood: fade to black

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic, modern and upbeat.
**Audio summary:** one restrained instrumental arc, with small sound accents aligned to the map reveal, UI highlights and final brand lockup.
